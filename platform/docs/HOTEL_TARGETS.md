# Hotel targets — who to approach first

**270 hotels in 32 destinations** for HotelBridge to approach for direct B2B rates. Built 31 Oct 2026 from web searches (sources at the end).

- **These are targets, not partners.** No hotel here has agreed to anything and no rates are implied. Never show them to agents as "available" until a contract is signed and loaded in `/admin`.
- **Rooms** = total rooms where a venue directory lists it (to check on the hotel's own site). Bigger hotels — 250 to 2,500 rooms — sign yearly fixed-rate contracts most easily: start there for Smart and Premium.
- **Checked ✓** = the hotel name came up in this week's searches. **verify** = well known, but not seen in these searches — confirm the exact name and brand before writing.
- **Contacts are not included on purpose.** Use each hotel's official website or its brand's sales office; record who you spoke to in the tracker.
- **The working tracker is [`hotel-targets.csv`](hotel-targets.csv)** — open it in Excel / Google Sheets and fill status, contact, next step and rate type as you go.

## How to approach each tier

| Tier | Who to ask | What to ask for | What wins them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ultra-premium** (Khyber, Taj Lake Palace, Oberoi, Aman, One&Only…) | Director of Sales / Revenue Manager; for brand hotels also the brand's India sales office (IHCL, Oberoi, Marriott, Hilton, Accor all have India teams) | Commissionable rate (often 10–15%) or static B2B net, on-request booking, honeymoon/wedding packages, permission to distribute to agents | Small curated agent network, no rate undercutting, prepaid bookings, real demand data (dates, room nights) |
| **Luxury 5★** | Sales manager (hotel) + brand India office | Static net by season, small allotment with release days, group rates | Volume promise you can keep, fast payment, Group Desk enquiries in clean format |
| **Premium 4–5★** (families, groups) | Hotel sales / revenue manager; owner for independents | Net rates + allotment, group rates, child policy, Indian/veg meals | Weekly volume, coach groups, low cancellation rate |

**Brand hotels** (Taj/IHCL, Oberoi, ITC, Marriott, Hilton, Accor, Hyatt, Radisson, Anantara, Centara): one conversation with the brand's India/regional sales office can open many properties. Independents (Khyber, Suryagarh, Evolve Back, Amangalla…) are negotiated one by one.

**First 30 days — suggested order:** P1 destinations first (Thailand ×3, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Singapore, Bali, Maldives, Mauritius, Vietnam, Kashmir, Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa, Kerala, Mumbai, Delhi). In each, start with 2 premium family/group hotels (volume) and 1 icon (brand) — about 40 conversations.
